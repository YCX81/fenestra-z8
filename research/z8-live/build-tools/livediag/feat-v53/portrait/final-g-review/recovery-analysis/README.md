The exact G final image is `c14894087d5fd03b0124c7d94f6cd521e94def58831a087738145ef91b60fcd2`. These are observations and bounded exclusions, not a passing whole-system result.

- The final PB image's seven shared reader calls target the exact final Live ELF exports. `test_pb_frame_arm.py` executes the final inlined PB frame instructions plus the actual reader: elapsed 0/71182/71183/3132052/3203235 us selects 0/0/1/44/EOF. - F and G saved drivers have identical contact sequences, Ready conditions and waits. Changes are image/model identity, the counter-clock model flag and ABI evidence inventory. Neither driver nor its capture probe issues a power request. The 900-second budget is host wall time; failure cleanup stops owned QEMU after failure. Return-half/release are relative 0.5/10-second intervals, not absolute end-of-run contacts.
- F return-half/release occurred at QOM 135.344383812/135.843692909 s. G occurred at 137.585938217/138.086798182 s: about 2.24 s later, not 12 s. In G the original MCU consumer at `40d271e4` received the expected half-key state at 137.637110022 s, within the 0.5-second hold, and returned matched state at `410221d4`. Thus this was not a missing external half input or a missed MCU reception. It does not prove the GUI handled that input.
- In G only one preview frame was queued (`frames=1`), and no preview frame was rendered by the GUI (`rendered_frames=0`); throughout checkpoints 117–120 `audio_pending=0`, `audio_stage=4`, and `audio_stop=0`. By checkpoint 121, `playing=0`, `audio_stage=0`, and `audio_gate=0`. After half release the native current photo id remains nonzero, whereas F has returned to id 0. This excludes persistent PB audio ownership at that checkpoint; it does not explain the GUI non-return.
- Parent/playback independently observed native UI event 7 (system shutdown) at native 150745192 us, approximately QOM 148.333269 s. The recorded G half press preceded that by about 10.75 s. There is no measured 30-second-idle threshold or identified shutdown producer in this analysis. Do not infer that increasing a timer or relaxing Ready resolves the defect.
- Existing PB snapshots omit `start_us`, `audio_t`, `last_frame`, preview duration, render-request, display readers, GUI present count and decode errors. Those fields are the minimum discriminating additions for another already-authorized lane. No running QMP/GDB was accessed and no firmware/model/fixture/driver source was changed here.
